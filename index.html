WILDLIFE & BTR 3 National Parks in BTR

National Parks of BTC / BTR

Biodiversity hotspots across Bodoland: UNESCO sites, vital corridors, and protected fauna.

1. Manas National Park

UNESCO World Heritage Site (1985) • Tiger & Biosphere Reserve
Districts Baksa & Chirang (BTR) (Extends to Bhutan as Royal Manas)
Famous For Contiguous transboundary landscape along Manas River, tall wet alluvial grasslands, Project Tiger, and Project Elephant.
Flagship Species Pygmy Hog Golden Langur Hispid Hare Bangal Florican
Other Wildlife One-horned Rhinoceros, Royal Bengal Tiger, Wild Water Buffalo, Asian Elephant, Clouded Leopard.

2. Raimona National Park

Assam's 6th National Park (Declared June 2021)
District Kokrajhar (Gossaigaon sub-division, BTR)
Famous For Dense riparian semi-evergreen Sal forest, Ripu Reserve Forest belt, bounded by Sankosh & Saralbhanga rivers, contiguous with Bhutan's Phibsoo Wildlife Sanctuary.
Flagship Species Gee's Golden Langur Asian Elephant Clouded Leopard
Other Wildlife Bengal Tiger, Gaur (Indian Bison), Dhole (Wild Dog), Spotted Deer (Chital), 4 Hornbill species.

3. Sikhna Jwhwlao National Park

Assam's 8th & BTR's 3rd National Park (Notified 2025)
Districts Kokrajhar & Chirang (BTR) (~316.29 sq. km area)
Famous For Named after legendary Bodo warrior Sikhna Jwhwlao (Joulia Dewan). Covers the historic Ultapani Reserve Forest and Saralpara belt along Saralbhanga River. Connects Raimona and Manas into a unified corridor.
Flagship Species 460+ Butterfly Species Golden Langur Great Indian Hornbill
Other Wildlife Asian Elephant (vital elephant corridor), Royal Bengal Tiger, Clouded Leopard, Gaur, rare orchids & medicinal flora.
Quick Exam Takeaways:
  • Total NPs in BTR: 3 (Manas, Raimona, Sikhna Jwhwlao).
  • Total NPs in Assam: 8 (Kaziranga, Manas, Dibru-Saikhowa, Nameri, Orang, Raimona, Dihing Patkai, Sikhna Jwhwlao).
  • Butterfly Capital: Ultapani inside Sikhna Jwhwlao NP is known as a haven for 460+ butterfly species.
  • Cultural Heritage: Sikhnajhar in Ultapani is regarded as a sacred spiritual site where traditional annual Bathou and Kherai Puja take place.

4. Culture & Festivals
  • Rongali / Bohag Bihu (April): Assamese New Year & harvest festival.
  • Bhogali / Magh Bihu (Jan): Harvest festival celebrated with bonfires (Meji).
  • Kongali / Kati Bihu (Oct): Solemn festival for crops protection & prosperity.
  • Sattriya Dance: Sangeet Natak Akademi recognized Classical Dance of India.
  • Satras & Namghars: Neo-Vaishnavite religious & cultural institutional centers.
  • Mekhela Chador: Traditional indigenous women attire.
  • Gamosa: Traditional handwoven cloth, symbol of honor & identity.
  • Majuli: World's largest inhabited freshwater river island.
  • Ambubachi Mela: Annual fertility festival at Kamakhya Temple (June).
  • Ali-Aye-Ligang: Spring agricultural festival of the Mising tribe.
  • Baishagu (mid-april): Bwisagu is one of the most popular seasonal festivals of the Bodos of Assam
  • Kherai:Kherai is the greatest religious festival of Boros.

HARDWARE & OS PC Optimization

Basic Solutions to Make Your PC Run Faster

Simple upgrades and smart habits can dramatically improve your PC performance.

Why Your PC Gets Slow
Low RAM: Not enough memory to handle concurrent tasks.
HDD Instead of SSD: Slower data read/write speeds choke the OS.
Low Disk Space: Full system drive slows down caching & swap files.
Too Many Background Apps: Consumes CPU, RAM, and system resources.
Dust & Overheating: Triggers thermal throttling and performance drops.
UPGRADE YOUR PC — BIGGEST PERFORMANCE BOOST

Upgrade RAM

More RAM = Smoother Multitasking
  • 4GB → Basic Use
  • 8GB → Best for Most Users
  • 16GB+ → Heavy Work / Gaming
Reduces lag & boosts overall speed

Use SSD Instead of HDD

SSD is 5x to 10x faster than HDD
  • Faster Boot Time
  • Faster App Loading
  • Snappy System Responsiveness
Install SSD for OS & primary apps

Keep C Drive Free

Low disk space throttles your OS
  • Keep at least 15–20% free space
  • Move bulky downloads/files to D drive
  • Uninstall unused software
More free space = Better performance

Manage Startup Apps

Too many startup apps delay boot times
  • Disable unwanted startup apps in Task Manager
  • Keep only essential services enabled
  • Boot time will be noticeably faster
Faster boot + Lower background load

Keep PC Cool & Clean

Overheating = Thermal Throttling
  • Clean out dust fans and filters regularly
  • Ensure unobstructed intake/exhaust airflow
  • Reapply quality thermal paste periodically
Cool PC runs faster and lasts longer
SMART HABITS — SMALL STEPS, BIG DIFFERENCE
Scan for Malware

Run regular scans to keep system clean & secure.

Clear Temp Files

Clean %temp% to free storage and reduce clutter.

Keep Windows Updated

Installs vital performance & security patches.

Update Drivers

Keep GPU & chipset drivers fresh for stability.

Restart Occasionally

Flushes system RAM leaks and clears temp cache.

Extra Quick Tips:
  • Use a lightweight, reliable Antivirus
  • Switch to 'High Performance' power plan in Windows
  • Avoid opening hundreds of idle browser tabs
  • Maintain regular backups for critical files
Best Upgrade Priority: SSD Upgrade → Add More RAM → Keep Drive Clean → Smart Habits
