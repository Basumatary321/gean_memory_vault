📐 Quantitative Aptitude & Math Sheet

Comprehensive formula collection for competitive examinations.

1. Percentage
Basic Percentage: $$\text{Percentage} = \frac{\text{Value}}{\text{Total Value}} \times 100$$
Increase / Decrease %: $$\text{\% Change} = \frac{\text{Difference}}{\text{Original Value}} \times 100$$
$P$ percent of $x$: $$P\% \text{ of } x = \frac{P}{100} \times x$$
2. Profit & Loss
$$\text{Profit} = SP - CP \quad | \quad \text{Loss} = CP - SP$$
$$\text{Profit \%} = \frac{\text{Profit}}{CP} \times 100 \quad | \quad \text{Loss \%} = \frac{\text{Loss}}{CP} \times 100$$
$$SP = \frac{100 + \text{Profit \%}}{100} \times CP \quad | \quad CP = \frac{100}{100 + \text{Profit \%}} \times SP$$
$$\text{Discount \%} = \frac{\text{Discount}}{MP} \times 100$$
Successive Discounts ($x\%$ and $y\%$): $$SP = MP \times \left(\frac{100 - x}{100}\right) \times \left(\frac{100 - y}{100}\right)$$ $$\text{Equivalent Discount} = \left(x + y - \frac{xy}{100}\right)\%$$
Legend: $SP$ = Selling Price, $CP$ = Cost Price, $MP$ = Marked Price
3. Simple & Compound Interest
Simple Interest (SI): $$SI = \frac{P \times R \times T}{100}, \quad \text{Amount} = P + SI$$
Compound Interest (CI): $$CI = P \left(1 + \frac{R}{100}\right)^T - P$$
Varying Annual Rates ($R_1\%, R_2\%, R_3\%$): $$\text{Amount} = P \left(1 + \frac{R_1}{100}\right)\left(1 + \frac{R_2}{100}\right)\left(1 + \frac{R_3}{100}\right)$$
Legend: $P$ = Principal, $R$ = Rate per annum, $T$ = Time in years
4. Average
Consecutive natural numbers up to $n$: $$\text{Avg} = \frac{n + 1}{2}$$
Consecutive even numbers up to $n$: $$\text{Avg} = \frac{n}{2} + 1$$
Consecutive odd numbers up to $n$: $$\text{Avg} = \frac{n + 1}{2}$$
First $n$ consecutive even numbers: $$\text{Avg} = n + 1$$
First $n$ consecutive odd numbers: $$\text{Avg} = n$$
5. Speed, Time & Distance
$$\text{Speed} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Time}}, \quad \text{Time} = \frac{\text{Distance}}{\text{Speed}}$$
Average Speed (Equal Distances at speeds $A$ & $B$): $$\text{Avg Speed} = \frac{2AB}{A + B}$$
Train crossing a pole: $$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Length of Train}}{\text{Speed}}$$
Train crossing a platform: $$\text{Time} = \frac{\text{Length of Train} + \text{Length of Platform}}{\text{Speed}}$$
6. Time & Work
$$\text{Work} = \text{Rate} \times \text{Time}$$
If $A$ does work in $X$ days, 1-day work is $\frac{1}{X}$.
$A$ (in $n$ days) and $B$ (in $m$ days) together take: $$\text{Days} = \frac{n \cdot m}{n + m}$$
Inlet pipe $A$ fills in $x$ hrs, outlet pipe $B$ empties in $y$ hrs ($y > x$): $$\text{Net Part Filled in 1 hr} = \frac{1}{x} - \frac{1}{y}$$
Work Equivalence Formula: $$\frac{M_1 D_1 H_1}{W_1} = \frac{M_2 D_2 H_2}{W_2}$$
7. Conversions & Units
$$1 \text{ km} = 1000 \text{ m} \quad | \quad 1 \text{ m} = 100 \text{ cm}$$
$$1 \text{ mile} \approx 1.609 \text{ km}$$
$$\text{Miles to KM} = \text{miles} \times 1.609$$
$$\text{KM to Miles} = \frac{\text{KM}}{1.609}$$
$$1 \text{ inch} = 2.54 \text{ cm} \quad | \quad 1 \text{ foot} = 0.3048 \text{ m}$$
8. Ratio & Proportion
$$a : b = c : d \implies a \times d = b \times c$$
If $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{c}{d} = \frac{e}{f} = k$, then: $$\frac{a + c + e + \dots}{b + d + f + \dots} = k$$
Given $A : B$ and $B : C$: $$A : B : C = (A \times B) : (B \times B) : (B \times C)$$
Compound Ratios: $\frac{a}{b} = \frac{N_1}{D_1}, \frac{b}{c} = \frac{N_2}{D_2}, \frac{c}{d} = \frac{N_3}{D_3}$ $$a:b:c:d = N_1N_2N_3 : D_1N_2N_3 : D_1D_2N_3 : D_1D_2D_3$$
9. Mensuration (2D & 3D)
Square: Area = $a^2$, Perimeter = $4a$
Rectangle: Area = $l \times b$, Perimeter = $2(l + b)$
Circle: Area = $\pi r^2$, Circumference = $2\pi r$
Triangle: Area = $\frac{1}{2} b h$, Perimeter = $a + b + c$
Cube: Volume = $a^3$, Surface Area = $6a^2$
Cuboid: Volume = $l \times b \times h$, Surface Area = $2(lb + bh + hl)$
Cylinder: Volume = $\pi r^2 h$, Curved Surface Area = $2\pi r h$
Sphere: Volume = $\frac{4}{3}\pi r^3$, Surface Area = $4\pi r^2$
Cone: Volume = $\frac{1}{3}\pi r^2 h$, CSA = $\pi r l$ ($l = \sqrt{r^2 + h^2}$)
Polygons: Sum of interior angles = $(n - 2) \times 180^\circ$
Each interior angle (regular $n$-gon) = $\frac{(n - 2) \times 180^\circ}{n}$
10. Number Systems
Natural Numbers: $1, 2, 3, 4, \dots, \infty$
Whole Numbers: $0, 1, 2, 3, \dots, \infty$
Integers: $\dots, -3, -2, -1, 0, 1, 2, 3, \dots$
Rational Numbers: Representable in form $\frac{p}{q}$ where $q \neq 0$ (e.g., $\frac{5}{1}, \frac{2}{7}, -\frac{1}{5}$).
Irrational Numbers: Cannot be expressed as $\frac{p}{q}$ (non-terminating, non-repeating decimals like $\sqrt{2}, \pi$).
